Shopping notes

Two similar finds in your Superbuy spreadsheet? Compare these details first

2026-10-23 · 3 min read

A tidy way to compare variants, measurements and unanswered questions when two listings look almost identical.

Two nearly identical jackets, two different prices, and a spreadsheet row that says “decide later.” That is where a shortlist tends to get stuck. The useful question is which listing gives you enough information to make a decision you can explain later.

Give each option its own row

Keep both source URLs in your Superbuy spreadsheet, even if the photos look the same. Add the seller name, selected color and size beside each link. A screenshot of a listing can help you remember what caught your eye, but it cannot replace the original source.

Try a simple note such as “black, size M, measurements still missing.” It is much easier to revisit than “nice jacket.” If the page changes, your note also tells you what needs checking again.

Compare the same measurement

A size label alone makes a weak comparison. Look for garment measurements and the method used to take them. Flat chest width and a full chest circumference describe different things; do not put them in one column as though they were interchangeable.

A garment you already like can provide a useful reference when you measure it the same way. Record the reference measurement, the listing measurement and any uncertainty separately. A small difference may matter differently depending on the cut and how you prefer to wear it.

Keep the cheaper option honest

Write down the currency and date beside the product price. Keep domestic shipping and later parcel costs in separate fields. Until those details are known, call the total an estimate. A lower listing price is only one part of the comparison.

Leave a question, not a guess

If one listing has a clearer size chart and the other has a better photo, write that down. You can mark one “needs measurement” and the other “needs variant confirmation” without choosing either yet. An unresolved question is useful information.

For fresh options, browse the Superbuy spreadsheet on Fashionreps and bring only the finds worth checking into your own shortlist. Keeping a smaller set of well-documented options makes the next decision much easier.
