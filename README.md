# Superbuy Spreadsheet — Compare Before You Order

A practical companion to the [Fashionreps Superbuy spreadsheet](https://fashion-reps.net/superbuy-spreadsheet), focused on comparing product information, checking fit evidence and preparing a realistic order budget.

**[Browse the Superbuy catalog →](https://fashion-reps.net/superbuy-spreadsheet)** · **[Read the independent English guide](https://superbuysheet.github.io/Superbuy-Spreadsheet/)**

## Start with the exact item

Record the seller URL, listing identifier if available, color, size, quantity and date checked. Reopen the source before ordering. Similar titles or photos do not prove that two listings offer the same item.

Select Superbuy using the Fashionreps agent selector or its **Use Superbuy for product links** control. Confirm the destination product and agent before paying. Orders, payments and shipping are completed on the agent's website.

## Compare evidence, not just prices

| Question | Useful evidence | Keep unresolved when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is this the right variant? | Exact source URL and selected options | A photo or title does not identify the offered variant |
| Will the fit work? | Consistent measurements compared with a similar item you own | Only a size label or unclear chart is available |
| What arrived? | Relevant warehouse photos and requested measurements | Important details are hidden or the photos show another variant |
| What is the full cost? | Item, delivery, fee and shipping amounts in one currency | A necessary cost is still unknown |
| Which route is suitable? | A current quote for your parcel and destination | Eligibility or packaging assumptions are unclear |

For clothing, distinguish flat widths from circumferences. For shoes, clarify the measuring method. Ask for missing measurements rather than relying on a universal size conversion.

## Two offers need the same assumptions

To compare two listings or shipping options, use the same quantity, variant, currency and destination. Record what each price includes and which amounts are estimates. A lower product price can still result in a higher delivered cost.

Use these cost headings in your own worksheet: items; seller-to-warehouse delivery; applicable purchasing or service fees; international shipping; payment/currency costs; applicable taxes or duties. Leave unknowns visibly marked rather than entering zero.

## Before approving onward shipping

- Match the received items with the ordered colors, sizes and quantities.
- Review visible condition, seams, closures and relevant measurements.
- Ask for additional photos when they can resolve a specific question.
- Confirm storage and return deadlines in your account.
- Compare current route eligibility, billable weight, dimensions and packaging options.

QC images provide limited visual evidence. They do not establish authenticity, material composition or long-term durability. Consolidation does not guarantee cheaper shipping, and smaller packaging may offer less protection.

## Example comparison note

“Listing A has a clear measurement chart; Listing B needs sleeve measurements. Prices recorded in the same currency. Domestic delivery differs. International shipping estimate pending.” This is an illustrative method, not a tested product recommendation or completed purchase.
