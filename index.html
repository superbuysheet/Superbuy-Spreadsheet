Use the directory as a research starting point

The Fashionreps Superbuy spreadsheet is a web-based catalog. This companion helps you compare the information behind a find rather than treating the listing as a recommendation.

Save the seller URL, listing identifier when available, color, size, quantity and date checked. Reopen the source before placing an order. Similar titles or photos do not establish that two listings offer the same item.

This is a practical guide, not an Excel download or a mirrored product database. Confirm current product details and agent terms when ordering.

Check the selected shopping agent

  1. Open the Superbuy directory on Fashionreps.
  2. Use Use Superbuy for product links or choose Superbuy in the shopping-agent selector.
  3. Check the selection before opening a purchase link, including after changing categories.
  4. On the destination site, confirm the item, variant, quantity and current terms before paying.

Orders, payments and shipping are handled by your selected agent. Reading a Superbuy page does not necessarily change a previously saved agent preference.

Compare two finds on equal terms

Before comparing prices, make sure you are comparing the intended variants. Use the same quantity, currency and destination, and record what each price includes.

Keep a missing detail marked as unresolved. Ask the seller or agent a specific question before turning uncertainty into a purchasing decision.

Make fit comparisons measurable

Measure a similar item that fits you and use the seller's method. Keep centimeters and inches consistent. For tops, distinguish flat chest width from circumference and note the measuring points for length and sleeves.

For pants, compare waist, rise, thigh and inseam, clarifying whether the waistband is relaxed or stretched. For shoes, ask which measurements are available and how they were taken; printed size labels alone are insufficient.

A size-tag photo cannot resolve an unclear measurement chart. Request the missing measurement rather than relying on a universal conversion.

Keep the delivered-cost assumptions visible

Use this worksheet for each option. Enter actual amounts when known, and label estimates and unknowns clearly.

Item price × quantity
+ seller-to-warehouse delivery
+ applicable purchasing or service fees
+ international shipping estimate
+ payment and currency-conversion charges
+ applicable taxes or duties
= estimated delivered total

Confirm currency, route, parcel dimensions and billable weight with the agent. A reference price in a catalog is not a shipping quote. If an important amount is missing, the comparison remains incomplete.

Compare route estimates using the same parcel assumptions. If one quote assumes smaller packaging, note how that affects protection as well as cost.

Review the received item and the proposed parcel

  • Match warehouse items with the ordered colors, sizes, quantities and source listings.
  • Review visible condition, relevant seams, closures and requested measurements.
  • Ask for another angle or close-up when it can resolve a specific question.
  • Check current storage and return deadlines in your account.
  • Confirm destination eligibility, packaging options and the actual billable-weight method before shipping.
QC photos provide limited visual evidence. Lighting affects apparent color, and photos alone cannot establish authenticity, material composition or long-term durability. Consolidation does not guarantee lower shipping costs.

An example comparison note

This is an illustrative method, not a completed purchase or a tested product recommendation.

“Listing A has a clear measurement chart; Listing B needs sleeve measurements. Prices recorded in one currency. Domestic delivery differs. International shipping estimate pending.”

The next step is to resolve the missing measurement and delivery assumptions. Once warehouse evidence and a parcel quote are available, update the same comparison rather than starting with unexplained new numbers.

Common questions

Is this official Superbuy documentation?

No. This independent guide links to Fashionreps. Platform and brand names identify services or listings without implying endorsement.

Does the catalog price include international shipping?

Treat it as a reference item price. Confirm delivery, fees, shipping and other applicable costs on the agent's site.

Does every listing have QC photos?

Check the individual listing for available evidence. Coverage is not guaranteed, and a photo set may not answer your fit or quality question.

Should I choose the cheapest shipping estimate?

Compare eligibility, parcel assumptions, packaging and current terms first. This guide does not recommend a universal route or guarantee delivery time.

What if a link is outdated?

Check the source and report the URL, expected result, actual result and date checked through the repository's correction link. Do not include private order or account details.
